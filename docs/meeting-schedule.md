## Regular meetings

| Meeting | When | Where | Who | Format |
| ------- | ---- | ----- | --- | ------ |
| Group meeting | _Day, time_ | _Room / video link_ | Everyone | One or two research updates |
| Subgroup meeting | _Day, time_ | _Room_ | _Subgroup_ | Informal data discussion |
| Journal club | _Day, time (biweekly)_ | _Room_ | Everyone | One paper, presenter leads discussion |
| One-on-one with PI | Scheduled individually | — | — | — |

- About 20 minutes plus questions
- Send slides to the group channel the day before, if possible
- Include a "what I need help with" slide
