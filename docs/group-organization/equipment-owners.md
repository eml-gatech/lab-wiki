# Equipment owners

Each shared instrument has a primary owner and a backup. Owners train new
users, keep the tutorial page up to date, and are the first contact when
something breaks.

!!! danger "Training first"
    Do not use an instrument until its owner (or backup) has signed you off.

## Owner responsibilities

- Train and sign off new users
- Keep the instrument's tutorial page accurate after any change to the
  hardware, software, or procedure
- Track consumables and service contracts
- Report breakdowns to the group and arrange repairs
- Find and train a replacement before leaving the group

## Becoming an owner

Ownership usually passes to whoever uses an instrument most. When you take one
over, update the table above and read through its tutorial page with the
outgoing owner.
