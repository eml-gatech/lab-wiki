# Mentors

Every new member is paired with a more senior member for their first
semester (or longer). The mentor is the first person to ask about lab
practice, safety, and "who do I talk to about…" questions.

## Current pairings

| Mentee | Mentor | Started | Focus |
| ------ | ------ | ------- | ----- |
| _New member_ | _Senior member_ | _Month YYYY_ | _e.g. thin-film fabrication, XRD training_ |
| _New member_ | _Senior member_ | _Month YYYY_ | |

## What mentees can expect

- A tour of the lab and introductions to equipment owners in the first week
- Help planning the first set of training sessions (see
  [equipment owners](equipment-owners.md))
- A regular check-in, e.g. 30 minutes every two weeks

## What mentors are expected to do

- Make sure the mentee has completed required safety training before working
  in the lab
- Point the mentee to the right [equipment tutorials](../equipment-tutorials/index.md)
  and owners rather than training them on instruments they don't own
- Flag problems early to the PI

??? question "What if the pairing isn't working?"
    Talk to the PI or the wiki maintainer. Re-pairing is normal and not a
    judgment on either person.
